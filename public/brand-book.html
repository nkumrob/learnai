01 — Brand story

Proof over hype.

Everyone says “learn AI.” Almost no one shows you how — or proves you did.

LearnAI is the calm, honest alternative to AI hype. A guided climb from complete beginner to genuine, demonstrable capability: five levels, fifty hands-on lessons, no signup, no fluff.

The thesis: you don’t finish a lesson by reading it — you finish it by doing it, and proving it. Every lesson ends with a real task and a quick check. Finish a level and you earn a certificate that means something, because you earned it by doing.

The one-liner

Learn useful AI skills. Then prove it.

What we stand for

  • Practical over theoretical
  • Proof over promises
  • Calm and credible, never loud
  • Free and open to everyone

02 — Who it’s for

Capable beginners.

People who’ve heard of AI, may have barely used it, and want real, work-relevant wins — not a research seminar.

A

The Curious Professional

Hears “learn AI” everywhere and feels behind. Wants concrete wins at work this week, without the jargon.

B

The Career Switcher

Needs credible, demonstrable skills — and a certificate they can actually show and verify.

C

The Builder-Curious

Non-technical but ambitious: wants to automate real work and build with no-code tools, agents, and APIs.

04 — Construction & clear space

Built on a grid.

Clear space

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the crossbar (the “x”) on every side. Never crowd it with text, edges, or other marks.

Minimum size

Mark: 16 px (favicon). Full lockup: 96 px wide. Below that, use the mark alone.

08 — Voice

Plainspoken & credible.

Principles

  • Plain, not jargon. Say the simple thing.
  • Encouraging, not cheesy. Calm confidence.
  • Show, don’t lecture. Examples over theory.
  • Proof over promises. Earn the claim.
We say

“Pass each lesson to unlock the next.” · “Earn a certificate you can verify.” · “Here’s a prompt you can steal.”

We don’t

“Unlock the future of AI!” · “All-in-one revolutionary platform.” · “Master AI in 5 minutes.”
